### Step 11 Limitations & Your Turn
- **Step 11 Limitation**: `put-scheduled-action` returned `UnsupportedOperation` for `usms-enrolment-morning-scale-out` and `usms-enrolment-evening-scale-in`[cite: 40]. The emulator build does not support scheduled scaling actions[cite: 13, 75]. On real AWS, `put-scheduled-action` updates `MinCapacity` and `MaxCapacity` bounds on a recurring cron schedule using the target timezone (`Asia/Thimphu`) to pre-provision capacity before expected traffic spikes[cite: 39, 40].
- **Step 11 "Your Turn"**: An `at()` scheduled action remains stored in the Application Auto Scaling backend after firing and does not self-delete[cite: 41]. Production runbooks must explicitly execute `aws application-autoscaling delete-scheduled-action` to clean up one-off schedules after they run[cite: 41, 42].
